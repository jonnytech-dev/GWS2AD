How It Works

A quick guide to what {{ app_name }} does and how to use it.

The big idea

Google Workspace is the master list. Active Directory is a copy of it.

Google Workspace Where your changes are saved
Sync You press the button
Active Directory Updated to match Google

A sync only goes one way, from Google to AD. AD never changes anything in Google.

Day to day

Four steps, in this order.

  1. 1
    Make your changes

    Add, edit, or delete users and groups on the Dashboard. They are saved to Google Workspace right away.

  2. 2
    Check the sync box

    It says Synced when AD matches Google. It says Out of Sync when AD is behind.

  3. 3
    Preview the sync

    Click Sync Workspace to AD. You see everything that will be created, updated, moved, or deleted. Nothing changes yet.

  4. 4
    Confirm

    Apply the sync. If new accounts were created, copy the temporary passwords shown. They appear only once.

What a sync does

Changes made to AD

  • Creates users and groups that are in Google but not in AD, plus any missing OUs.
  • Updates user details and group descriptions.
  • Moves users into the OU that matches their Google OU.
  • Adds and removes group members so each group matches Google exactly.
  • Deletes users and groups that are in AD but not in Google, unless they are on the Ignore list. Last, it deletes OUs that Google no longer has, but only empty ones.

Good to know

Safety and small details

  • Backup first. A copy of your Google data is saved before each sync. If the backup fails, the sync still runs.
  • Ignore list. In the preview, click Keep in AD on anything that should stay in AD only, and say why if you like. Sync will not delete it. This works for users, groups and OUs. Ignored items move to the bottom of the preview, where Stop ignoring undoes it. Settings has an Ignore list tab with all of them.
  • Email notifications. The small switch under the sync box decides if new people are emailed their username and temporary password. Hover over it for details.
  • Your role. What you can do depends on your role. It is listed in Settings, under Profile.
  • Changes made elsewhere. Changes made directly in the Google Admin console can take up to 10 minutes to show here. Running a sync refreshes the lists right away.
  • Two-step sign-in. After your password, enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app. Lost your phone? Sign in with a recovery code, then pair your new phone in Settings, under Profile.
  • Lockout. Five wrong passwords, or five wrong codes, lock the account for 15 minutes.
  • Auto sign-out. You are signed out after 15 minutes of no activity.
